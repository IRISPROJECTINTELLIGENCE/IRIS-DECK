# Financial model

`IRIS_model_5y.xlsx` is the five-year driver model behind the deck's market and model slide (slide 11) and appendix A5–A6. It is generated by `build_model.py`:

```
python3 build_model.py IRIS_model_5y.xlsx
```

then recalculated (LibreOffice, via the xlsx skill's `recalc.py`) so cached values are present. Every number outside the Inputs sheet is a formula. Three cases (Base, Upside, Conservative) run side by side; each input carries its unit, type and source on the Inputs sheet.

What it models (D-37, 4 November 2026): four revenue lines. **IRIS for Lenders**: a lender writes IRIS into facilities as a drawdown condition; the developer pays 15 bps a year on the full project debt stack ($80M average, so the $10,000 a month cap binds at $120K a year); each lender adds 0.8 new facilities a month after adoption and ten facilities from its existing book over 18 months as loans re-paper; 24-month terms; lenders at months 12, 18, 22, 26, 29, 32, 34, 36 and then about every two months to 20 at month 60. **IRIS for Builders**: $3,500 a month per live job, following the builder; two pilots paid from month 6, then a builder every four to eight months, each growing from two jobs to six. **IRIS for Developers**: developer-direct projects with no lender condition from month 30, $2,500 a month. **Assessments**: $35,000 per engagement (benchmarked to QS report fees), on 60% of on-platform distressed facilities (4% a year) plus off-platform wins ramping to eight a year. Costs scale by formula: deployment analysts one per eight live jobs, a lender success manager per two lenders, two engineers plus one per 1.5 lenders, fixed roles by start month, 8% commission on new facility fees, $4,000 to connect each facility, cloud $250 a month per live job, PI insurance to $500K, 5% contingency. Founders at $100K then $180K from Year 3. Funding: $500K pre-seed at month 1, $3M seed at month 15, Series A optional and shown separately.

Base case: revenue $0.19M / $2.3M / $9.4M / $22.9M / $40.8M; EBITDA −$0.33M / −$0.32M / $2.4M / $9.0M / $18.2M; run rate $49.5M at month 60; team 2 / 18 / 37 / 70 / 103; cash positive throughout with the seed (low $161K). Market $291M Australia, $55M Queensland. Upside $63M and conservative $7.6M of revenue in Year 5.

The lender adoption cadence and the per-lender facility rate are company targets, not sourced figures; the distress rate is an estimate. Any change to an assumption needs a decision in `../review/review-decisions.md`, and slide 11 and appendix A5–A6 move with the workbook.

`build_model_36m_legacy.py` and `IRIS_model_36m_legacy.xlsx` are the 36-month model of 1 November 2026 (D-33), kept for reference.
