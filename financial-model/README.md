# Financial model (controlled source)

`IRIS_model_36m.xlsx` is the 36-month driver model behind slides 2, 11, 12, A5 and A6 (14-slide deck, D-31). It is generated by `build_model.py`:

```
python3 build_model.py IRIS_model_36m.xlsx
```

Sheets: Read me, Inputs (every assumption with source and type), Summary, Market, Base, Conservative. Formulas everywhere except Inputs. After regenerating, recalculate and check the Summary sheet reads: total revenue 123,123 / 961,676 / 2,615,109 (base Y1–Y3) and 65,516 / 394,891 / 1,071,234 (conservative); total costs 499,749 / 962,222 / 1,742,949 (base); annualised revenue at month 36: 3,625,977 (base), 1,520,047 (conservative); $42,000 per paid builder project a year; $52,500 per facility at month 36; about $531,562 of facility fees per lender at month 36.

Key assumptions (base, D-33): builders are the wedge: two pilot builders paid from month 6, builders 3–5 from months 12, 20, 28; each starts with two live jobs at $3,500 a month and adds about one every two months to a cap of six (12 / 22 / 30 jobs at year ends); first lender condition at month 12, further lenders at months 18, 22, 26, 29, 32, 34, 36; 0.8 new facilities per lender a month, 24-month term, $80M average across the project debt stack at 15 bps (floor $3,500, cap $10,000 a month, so the cap binds at $120K a year); founders at $100K plus 12% super (D-21: the round is $500K at a $5M cap; Year 1 costs about $500K); hires from month 16 to 15 people at month 36; $3M seed at month 15 (base) or 18 (conservative). Conservative (the previous base, D-07): builders to five jobs from month 7, lenders at months 12, 21, 27, 33, 0.5 facilities a month on $60M at 12.5 bps, nine people.

Any change to an assumption needs a decision in `../review/review-decisions.md` before it is made, and slides 2, 11, 12, A5 and A6 must be updated together with the workbook. The base case is D-33 (1 Nov 2026). The conservative case is the previous base (D-07).
