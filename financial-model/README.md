# Financial model

`IRIS_model_5y.xlsx` is the five-year driver model behind the deck's market and model slide (slide 11), the round slide (slide 13) and appendix A5–A6. It is generated by `build_model.py`:

```
python3 build_model.py IRIS_model_5y.xlsx
```

then recalculated (LibreOffice, via the xlsx skill's `recalc.py`) so cached values are present. Every number outside the Inputs sheet is a formula. Three cases (Base, Upside, Conservative) run side by side; each input carries its unit, type and source on the Inputs sheet.

The workbook is the investor copy (D-47, 26 November 2026): its labels carry no decision numbers, no owner notes and no previous terms, so it can be sent as it is. Sheets in reading order: Read me, Summary, Market, Inputs, Base, Upside, Conservative. Diligence caveats are kept in `../review/review-decisions.md` (O-15, O-16, O-24), not in the workbook. It is formatted as plain Excel at the owner's direction (D-48): Calibri 11, black text, bold headings, a thin rule under headers and above totals, no fills and no coloured fonts. Keep that look.

What it models (D-37, 4 November 2026; terms of D-39, 24 November 2026): three revenue lines, for the two products and assessments. The developer-direct line of the 4 November build was removed on 26 November 2026 (D-48): there is no developer product. **IRIS for Lenders**: a lender writes IRIS into facilities as a drawdown condition; the developer pays 15 bps a year on the full project debt stack, senior debt alone or senior with mezzanine, above a floor of $3,500 a month, with no cap (D-51, 26 November 2026). The average facility is one figure for the market and the lender line (D-49, 26 November 2026): the average build contract times the debt multiple, a derived row on the Inputs sheet. Sized by the owner on 26 November 2026 (D-50): base $66.7M × 1.2 = $80M, which pays $120,000 a year; upside $85M × 1.2 = $102M, $153,000 a year; conservative $40M × 1.2 = $48M at 12.5 bps. Each lender adds 0.8 new facilities a month after adoption and ten facilities from its existing book over 18 months as loans re-paper; 24-month terms; lenders at months 12, 18, 22, 26, 29, 32, 34, 36 and then about one in two months to 20 at month 60. **IRIS for Builders**: $3,500 a month per live job, following the builder; two pilots paid from month 6, then a builder at intervals of four to eight months, each growing from two jobs to six. **Assessments**: $35,000 per engagement (benchmarked to QS report fees), on 60% of on-platform distressed facilities (4% a year) plus off-platform wins ramping to eight a year. Costs scale by formula: deployment analysts one per eight live jobs, a lender success manager per two lenders, two engineers plus one per 1.5 lenders, fixed roles by start month, 8% commission on new facility fees, $4,000 to connect each facility, cloud $250 a month per live job, PI insurance to $500K, 5% contingency. Founders at $100K then $180K from Year 3. Funding: $750K pre-seed SAFE at month 1, $3M seed at month 15, Series A optional and shown separately.

Base case: revenue $0.19M / $2.3M / $9.3M / $22.7M / $40.5M; EBITDA −$0.48M / −$0.31M / $2.4M / $8.9M / $18.1M; run rate $49.2M at month 60; team 2 / 18 / 36 / 69 / 102; cash positive throughout with the seed (lowest $261K in the base case, $159K in the conservative case). Market $274M Australia, $52M Queensland. Upside $78M and conservative $6.2M of revenue in Year 5; the conservative case turns EBITDA-positive in Year 5.

The round: Inputs section 9 holds the seven use-of-funds lines from slide 13 ($320K build, $224K founders, $46K security, cloud and models, $20K sales, $20K legal, $20K contingency and insurance, $100K reserve). They do not drive the model; Year 1 costs come from sections 6 and 7. The Summary sets the two side by side and bridges the round to cash at the end of Year 1: $750,000 + $183,300 of revenue − $665,858 of costs = $267,442. Year 1 costs run $15,858 above the $650K of spend lines because cloud use, onboarding and commissions arrive with the first paid jobs.

The lender adoption cadence and the per-lender facility rate are company targets; the distress rate is an estimate. Any change to an assumption needs a decision in `../review/review-decisions.md`, and slides 11 and 13 and appendix A5–A6 move with the workbook. After a regeneration, compare every numeric cell with the previous build before committing.
