# Financial model (controlled source)

`IRIS_model_36m.xlsx` is the 36-month driver model behind slides 2, 11, 12, A5 and A6 (14-slide deck, D-31). It is generated by `build_model.py`:

```
python3 build_model.py IRIS_model_36m.xlsx
```

Sheets: Read me, Inputs (every assumption with source and type), Summary, Market, Base, Conservative. Formulas everywhere except Inputs. After regenerating, recalculate and check the Summary sheet reads: total revenue 123,123 / 961,676 / 2,615,109 (base Y1–Y3) and 65,516 / 394,891 / 1,071,234 (conservative); total costs 499,749 / 962,222 / 1,742,949 (base); annualised revenue at month 36: 3,625,977 (base), 1,520,047 (conservative); $42,000 per paid builder project a year; $52,500 per facility at month 36; about $531,562 of facility fees per lender at month 36.

Key assumptions (base, D-32): builder subscriptions $3,500 a month per project, two R&D deployments from month 3 paid from month 6, then 1 / 1.5 / 2.5 new projects a month with 1% monthly loss (8 / 24 / 50 paid projects at year ends); first lender condition at month 12, further lenders at months 18, 22, 26, 29, 32, 34, 36; 1.5 new facilities per lender a month, 24-month term, $35M average at 15 bps (floor $3,500, cap $10,000 a month); founders at $100K plus 12% super (D-21, 30 Oct 2026: the round is $500K at a $5M cap; Year 1 costs $500K); hires from month 16 to 17 people at month 36; $3M seed at month 15 (base) or 18 (conservative). Conservative (the previous base, D-07): 1 / 1.25 / 1.5 builder adds, lenders at months 12, 21, 27, 33, 0.75 facilities a month, 12.5 bps on $30M, nine people.

Any change to an assumption needs a decision in `../review/review-decisions.md` before it is made, and slides 2, 11, 12, A5 and A6 must be updated together with the workbook. The base case is D-32 (proposed 1 Nov 2026, pending O-14): eight lenders by month 36 at 1.5 facilities a month each, builder adds 1.0 / 1.5 / 2.5 a month, 17 people. The conservative case is the previous base (D-07).
