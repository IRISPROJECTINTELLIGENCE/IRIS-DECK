# Financial model (controlled source)

`IRIS_model_36m.xlsx` is the 36-month driver model behind slides 14, 15, A4 and A5. It is generated by `build_model.py`:

```
python3 build_model.py IRIS_model_36m.xlsx
```

Sheets: Read me, Inputs (every assumption with source and type), Summary, Market, Base, Conservative. Formulas everywhere except Inputs. After regenerating, recalculate and check the Summary sheet reads: total revenue 123,123 / 961,676 / 2,615,109 (base Y1–Y3) and 65,516 / 394,891 / 1,071,234 (conservative); annualised revenue at month 36: 3,625,977 (base), 1,520,047 (conservative); $42,000 per paid builder project a year; $52,500 per facility at month 36; about $531,562 of facility fees per lender at month 36.

Key assumptions (base): builder subscriptions $3,500 a month per project, two R&D deployments from month 3 paid from month 6, then 1 / 1.25 / 1.5 new projects a month with 1% monthly loss (8 / 21 / 36 paid projects at year ends); first lender condition at month 12, further lenders at months 21, 27, 33; 0.75 new facilities per lender a month, 24-month term, $35M average at 15 bps (floor $3,500, cap $10,000 a month); founders at $140K plus 12% super; hires from month 16; $3M seed at month 15 (base) or 18 (conservative). Conservative: builders from month 7 at half the rate, first facility at month 18, 12.5 bps on $30M, later hires.

Any change to an assumption needs a decision in `../review/review-decisions.md` before it is made, and slides 14, 15, A4 and A5 must be updated together with the workbook. The builder-project ramp (8 / 21 / 36) is under review (O-01).
