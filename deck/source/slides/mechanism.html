Problem · the mechanism

04

The position sits in five systems and travels up by hand.
Each hand-off filters it. The lender sees it last.

1 · The project’s systems

Procore or Aconex, Jobpac, PayApps, ProcurePro and the inbox. Variations, delays, trades let and money owed each live somewhere different.

2 · The builder’s monthly report

Assembled by hand from those systems, by the party it reports on.

3 · The lender’s QS certificate

Work in place checked against the claim, from a site visit and the builder’s submissions. Not independently verified.

4 · The facility decision

Funded on the certificate, weeks after the records moved.

What the certificate does not carry is already in the records: notices at time bars, cost moving before it is notified, contingency spent, trades owed more than claimed. The last is usually the first sign.

Founder observation, from work inside builders across three delivery models. Row-by-row detail and the market shifts since 2021: appendix A2, A3.

The chain: five systems hold the records; the builder's team assembles the monthly report from them (the internal cost report and the PCG report to the client); the lender's quantity surveyor values work in place from a site inspection and checks the claim against the contract sum, working from the claim, the variation and EOT registers as submitted, the statutory declaration that trades are paid and the builder's programme, and reports that the information was not independently verified and that no allowance is made for defects; the lender funds the drawdown on that certificate. What sits beneath it, by cause: entitlement at the time bar; forecast movement not yet notified and the prelims it extends; how much of forecast final cost is still contingency and provisional sums; market exposure in the trades; completion risk (labour on site, open defects and non-conformances against PC); and cash strain in the trades, what subcontractors are owed against what the builder has claimed and been paid, usually the first sign of a builder in trouble and the one the lender sees last. Since 2021, fixed prices, thinner builders and in-house delivery have moved more of the risk inside the project while the lender's view stayed a certificate on the head contract (appendix A3).
