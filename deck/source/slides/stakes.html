Bathla figures from the administrator's updates as reported by The Urban Developer on 25 September 2026: about $3.08bn owed to secured lenders within about $3.4bn of total creditor debt; about $736m of inter-company receivables and payables that may be overstated and were still being reconciled a month after appointment. All 13 remaining active sites were closed on 23 September after emergency lender funding ran out. One lender's senior loan on a near-complete project was later sold and a receiver appointed to settle presales (ABC News, 17 October 2026). The replacement-builder premium of 20 to 40 per cent is founder experience, not a measured figure; part of it prices what the incoming builder cannot see in the records. Scope: IRIS reads a project's builder systems, so on a Bathla project it would show that project's trades owed against what was claimed and certified, and its cost position against forecast; the group's inter-company position is the administrator's reconciliation. Construction's share of insolvencies, 24.5 per cent in FY2025-26, is on slide 2 (ASIC).
