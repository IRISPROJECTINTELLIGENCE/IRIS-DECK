A2

Backs slide 4, which shows three lines of the lender’s QS report and what sits beneath each. This page gives all six causes, grouped under the same three lines. The report: a lender’s QS progress drawdown report typically carries a development cost table, work in place valued against the builder’s claim, programme and extensions of time, cost to complete, variations as submitted, approvals, insurances, the builder’s statutory declaration that subcontractors are paid, risk items and a cash flow forecast. The AIQS guidance note (Construction Financing Reports, 2nd edition, 2019) has the QS assess cost to complete on the footing that the original contractor completes the works. Reports commonly state that information supplied was not independently verified. The six causes. 1. Time bars: delay notices, variations, latent conditions and provisional-sum adjustments each carry a notice period; what is near or past its bar decides whether a cost is recoverable or the builder’s own, with liquidated damages on top. 2. Completion: labour on site against the programme, and open defects and rework against practical completion, show whether the date will be reached. 3. Cost not yet notified: the records show delays and changes coming before a notice is lodged, and each carries consequential cost, most visibly preliminaries that extend with the delay. 4. Soft forecast: much of a forecast final cost sits as contingency inside trade budgets and as allowances; how much is still soft is a risk measure in itself. 5. Trades still to let: rates fixed at tender are open to the market once labour and material prices move; scope still to let, or let against an allowance, is price risk still open. 6. Trades paid: with each claim the builder declares that its subcontractors have been paid what is owed (in Queensland, a supporting statement under section 75 of the BIF Act); the payment records show each trade’s claims, approvals and payments. ASIC found false declarations may signal cash flow problems (16-325MR, 2016). Basis: the report column from the AIQS guidance; the causes from the founders’ work inside builders.
