A6

The workbook runs five years in three cases, with inputs on one sheet and formulas everywhere else. Three revenue lines: IRIS for Lenders, IRIS for Builders and assessments; the developer-direct line of the 4 November build was removed on 26 November 2026 (D-48). Builder line: R&D deployments are unpaid until the position reconciles to the builder's own cost reports; paid builders are then added at the cadence on the slide, each starting with two jobs and growing to six, since builders usually move a subscription to their next job. Lender line: each lender with the condition adds 0.8 new facilities a month, plus ten re-papered over 18 months at extensions and refinances; facilities leave after a 24-month term; the fee is 15 bps on the facility limit with a floor of $3,500 and a cap of $10,000 a month. The average facility is the average build contract times the debt multiple, so the lender line and the market sizing on A5 price the same facility: $40M at 1.2 times in the base case ($48M), $61M at 1.2 times in the upside ($73M) and $40M at 1.0, senior debt alone, in the conservative case (owner's direction of 26 November 2026, D-49). People: founders from month 1 at $100K plus 12% superannuation; hires start after the seed round. Other costs: Year 1 matches the use of funds; cloud and model costs are $250 a month per live job; security certification in Year 1 then annual audits; insurance rises as lenders rely on it; contingency is 5% of costs from Year 2.
