A6

Backs the base case on slide 11. The workbook runs five years in three cases, with inputs on one sheet and formulas elsewhere. Lender line: lenders adopt at months 12, 18, 22, 26, 29, 32, 34 and 36, then roughly six a year to 20 at month 60. The cadence is a company target. Each lender with the condition adds 0.8 new facilities a month, plus ten re-papered from its existing book over 18 months at extensions and refinances; facilities leave after a 24-month term. The fee is 15 bps a year on the facility limit, with a floor of $3,500 and a cap of $10,000 a month. The average facility is the average build contract times the debt multiple, so this page and A5 price the same facility (D-49): $66.7M at 1.2 times in the base case ($80M), $85M at 1.2 times in the upside ($102M, with a $12,500 cap) and $40M at 1.2 times in the conservative case ($48M, at 12.5 bps) (D-50, 26 November 2026). The base facility pays the cap, $120,000 a year. Builder line: R&D deployments are unpaid until IRIS reconciles to the builder’s own cost reports; two pilots pay from month 6, then a new builder joins at intervals of four to eight months, each starting with two jobs and growing to six. Years 2 and 4: 3 and 14 lenders on 30 and 229 facilities; 22 and 58 paid builder jobs; 18 and 69 people. Assessments: from month 30, at $35,000, benchmarked to QS report fees. People: founders from month 1 at $100K plus 12% superannuation, then $180K from Year 3; hires start after the seed; analysts one per eight live jobs, a lender success manager per two lenders, two engineers plus one per 1.5 lenders. Other costs: Year 1 matches the use of funds on slide 13; cloud and model costs are $250 a month per live job; 8% commission on new facility fees; $4,000 to connect each facility; contingency is 5% of costs from Year 2. Funding: a $3M seed at month 15 in all cases; a Series A is optional and shown separately. Sensitivities, base case: the base facility sits at the cap, so a larger facility or a higher fee adds nothing until the cap moves; each $10M of average facility below $80M takes about $4.6M off Year 5 revenue, and each basis point of fee below 15 is about $2.5M in Year 5. Each mature lender is about $2.2M a year of fees (about 19 facilities); each paid builder job is $42K a year. Results: revenue $0.19M / $2.3M / $9.3M / $22.7M / $40.5M; EBITDA −$0.48M / −$0.31M / $2.4M / $8.9M / $18.1M. Upside $77M and conservative $6.2M in Year 5.
