A5

The workbook runs 36 months for both cases, with inputs on one sheet and formulas everywhere else. Builder line: R&D deployments are unpaid until the position reconciles to the builder's own cost reports; paid projects then grow by a monthly add rate with 1% monthly loss, since builders usually move a subscription to their next job. Lender line: each lender with the condition adds 0.75 facilities a month (new facilities, extensions and refinances, as loans are papered); facilities leave after a 24-month term; the fee is 15 bps on the facility limit with a floor of $3,500 and a cap of $10,000 a month. People: founders from month 1 at $100K plus 12% superannuation; hires start after the seed round. Other costs: Year 1 matches the use of funds; cloud and model costs are $1,000 a month fixed plus $150 per live job; security certification in Year 1 then annual audits; insurance rises from $10K to $45K a year as lenders rely on it; contingency is 5% of costs from Year 2. Cash: the round arrives in month 1 and the $3M seed in month 15 (base) or 18 (conservative); no R&D tax refund is counted.
