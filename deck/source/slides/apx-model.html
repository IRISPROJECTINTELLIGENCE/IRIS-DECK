A6

Backs the base case on slide 11. The workbook runs five years in three cases, with inputs on one sheet and formulas elsewhere. Lender line: lenders adopt at months 12, 18, 22, 26, 29, 32, 34 and 36, then roughly six a year to 20 at month 60. The cadence is a company target. Each lender with the condition adds 0.8 new facilities a month, plus ten re-papered from its existing book over 18 months at extensions and refinances; facilities leave after a 24-month term. The fee is 15 bps a year on the facility limit, with a floor of $3,500 a month and no cap (D-51). The facilities IRIS is written into average $80M in the base case, $102M in the upside and $48M in the conservative case (at 12.5 bps), set by the owner on 26 November 2026 (D-50) and entered directly. A5 sizes the market on the market's average job, a $40M contract or a $48M facility (D-52). Builder line: R&D deployments are unpaid until IRIS reconciles to the builder’s own cost reports; two pilots pay from month 6, then a new builder joins at intervals of four to eight months, each starting with two jobs and growing to six. Years 2 and 4: 3 and 14 lenders on 30 and 229 facilities; 22 and 58 paid builder jobs; 18 and 69 people. Assessments: from month 30, at $35,000, benchmarked to QS report fees. People: founders from month 1 at $100K plus 12% superannuation, then $180K from Year 3; hires start after the seed; analysts one per eight live jobs, a lender success manager per two lenders, two engineers plus one per 1.5 lenders. Other costs: Year 1 matches the use of funds on slide 13; cloud and model costs are $250 a month per live job; 8% commission on new facility fees; $4,000 to connect each facility; contingency is 5% of costs from Year 2. Funding: a $3M seed at month 15 in all cases; a Series A is optional and shown separately. Sensitivities, base case: each $10M of average facility is about $4.6M of Year 5 revenue; each basis point of fee is about $2.5M in Year 5. Each mature lender is about $2.2M a year of fees (about 19 facilities); each paid builder job is $42K a year. Results: revenue $0.19M / $2.3M / $9.3M / $22.7M / $40.5M; EBITDA −$0.48M / −$0.31M / $2.4M / $8.9M / $18.1M. Upside $78M and conservative $6.2M in Year 5.
