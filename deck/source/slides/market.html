Building work in scope (IBISWorld E3019 medium- and high-rise apartments, $33.5bn; E3021 commercial and industrial on jobs of $10M or more, about 65% of $70.9bn) is converted to value under construction by build period (29 months apartments, ABS; 24 months C&I, estimate), divided by an average contract of $40M (cross-checked against Qualitas, PRD and Hubexo figures) to give about 4,330 live projects, of which about 3,470 are developer-funded (IBISWorld major-market shares). Facility fee: 15 bps a year on a facility about the size of the build contract, within a $3,500 floor and $10,000 cap a month. Queensland shares: 19.6% of multi-unit work, 18.2% of C&I (IBISWorld). The obtainable figure is the month-36 annualised revenue of the base case (D-32): 8 lenders, 129 facilities, 50 paid builder projects. The previous base ($3.6M) is kept as the conservative case.
