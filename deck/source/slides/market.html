Bottom-up, from the two IBISWorld industries IRIS serves. In scope: medium- and high-rise apartments ($33.5bn a year, IBISWorld E3019) and commercial and industrial building on jobs of $10M or more (65% of $70.9bn, IBISWorld E3021; the share is our estimate). Each job is live for its build period (apartments 29 months per ABS; commercial 24 months, our estimate), which gives about $173bn under construction, or about 4,300 jobs at a $40M average. IBISWorld's major-markets data puts 88% of multi-unit and 73% of commercial work with developers, who carry a construction facility; the rest is end users and government, where the builder pays. Queensland is 19.6% of multi-unit work done and about 18% of commercial. The obtainable figure is the base case of the model at month 36, a run rate; Year 3 recognised revenue is lower because the base grows through the year. Recovery is described, not sized: engagement counts, fees and willingness to pay are unknown, and adding it would risk counting a project already in the facility-fee market. Insurers and surety providers are sized but not counted: there is no product for them in this round. Each input and its source is on the Inputs sheet of the workbook.
