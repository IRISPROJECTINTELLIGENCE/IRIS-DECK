IRIS

The builder’s systems

Time, cost and quality risk, for both the lender and builder.

In use in Australia unless marked. Procore, Aconex, Jobpac, PayApps and ProcurePro are systems IRIS reads. About sixty products reviewed, November 2026; sources in appendix A8.

Products by what they read and who they serve. IRIS sits in both cells of the right-hand column: IRIS for Lenders and IRIS for Builders read the same reconciled position, and it is the one name in the lender cell. Lender's QS: progress drawdown reports certify work in place against the claim; reports state the builder's information was not independently verified. IPEX (Melbourne): a project payment account; its Payment Verification Report (sponsored article, 13 November 2026) sets QS-certified progress against the payments made to each trade through the account and ranks the gap; the vendor says several lenders require the data as a condition of continued funding; it sees the payments that pass through its account and holds no cost, forecast, variation or programme data. Korrix Intelligence Partners (Sydney): Assess, Monitor (monthly progress meetings, site walks, covenant register, risk brief, proposed as a loan condition) and Protect; a service, no software integrations. CreditorWatch and Equifax iCIRT: credit files and ratings on the builder company; nothing on the project. Cintech: one record per project from thirteen named connectors (Aconex, Procore, ProcurePro, PayApps, Jobpac and others), cited answers, AI workers, time bars; founders ex-CIO and ex-infrastructure lead at Tier 2 builders; no customers named. Vyne: decision intelligence for builders; illustrative preview. Slate Technologies (California): a decision assistant that ingests the major construction platforms, Procore named, and links to the schedule; Tier 1 general contractor customers; a real-estate intelligence module aimed at banks and REITs as land buyers; no lender project-position product; no Australian customers found. Octant AI (Brisbane; BMD-backed): machine-learning outturn forecasts from cost reports and schedules; names financiers among users of its insights but has no lender product. Mastt (Sydney): capital-project controls for owners and their PM consultants; no builder-system integration, no lender features. Procore with Datagrid: agents over the builder's ERP and documents, for the builder. Lendbase: a commercial-lending origination marketplace, upstream of the facility; a channel rather than a competitor. Not on the slide: Built, Rabbet and Land Gorilla (US lender draw tools, no Australian presence found); SteelCap (a landing page; removed 7 November 2026 at the owner's direction); Deep Space and Plexa (all-in-one builder platforms that replace the systems); Alloovium and Trunk Tools (builder-side AI); BuildGuard AI (CSIRO research stage). IPEX is not a partner. Full review: research/competitive-landscape.md.
