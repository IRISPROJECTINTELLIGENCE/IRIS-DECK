As of November 2026 we found no product that reads a project’s systems and reconciles them into one position for the lender, the builder and the developer. About sixty products reviewed: appendix A8.

Korrix Intelligence Partners Pty Ltd, Sydney: three layers, Assess (builder financial health check from nine registers and reports, trade contractor assessment, pre-close scorecard), Monitor (monthly, attendance at progress meetings, site walks, covenant register, risk brief; proposed as a loan condition at a fixed monthly fee), Protect (insurance layer in development). Reports only to the lender; no relationship with the builder; no software integrations. SteelCap: a landing page, 'coming soon', early access programme. Cintech Construction Intelligence Platform: one record per project synced from thirteen named systems, cited answers, AI workers, time bars; founders ex-CIO and ex-infrastructure lead at Tier 2 builders; no customers named. Vyne: decision intelligence and organisational memory for builders; illustrative preview, no live systems. The lender's existing controls: the QS certificate, IPEX payment-account control (vendor-claimed lender mandates), project trust accounts, CreditorWatch and iCIRT ratings. IPEX is not a partner. The bounded claim: as of November 2026, no system found that reads a project's source systems and reconciles them into one position shared by lender, builder and developer.
