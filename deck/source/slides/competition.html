The bounded claim: as of October 2026, no system found that reconciles a construction project's source systems into one source-linked position shared by builder, developer and lender. Adjacent software is either a source system IRIS reads or a tool it sits beside. IPEX is the closest Australian control: payment-account control with vendor-claimed lender mandates; it moves money and does not read the position. Built is the closest overseas analogue: draw management for 300+ US lenders, reading Procore financials, checking the submitted package rather than reconciling beneath it. Builder-side AI (Procore and Datagrid agents, Document Crunch, Octant, Alloovium, Slate, Trunk Tools) works for the builder on the builder's data and shares nothing upward. The full review of about sixty products and firms is in research/competitive-landscape.md and appendix A8. IPEX is not a partner.
