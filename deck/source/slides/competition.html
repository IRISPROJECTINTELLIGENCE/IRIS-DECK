The claim is bounded: as of October 2026, after a search across construction platforms, lender-side tools and AI start-ups in Australia, the US and the UK, we found no product that reads across a project's systems and reports one reconciled, source-linked position to the builder, the developer and the lender, or that a lender writes into facility documents. That is a blue-ocean position, not a moat; the moat is built by being the standard lenders rely on and by the reconciled project data behind it. Source systems: Procore bought Datagrid (closed January 2026, US$168m), whose agents read ERPs and other systems; its owner tools, including funding-source tracking, are due for general release in late 2026. Trimble owns Jobpac through Viewpoint and bought Document Crunch (April 2026, US$246m) for contract and notice risk. Autodesk renamed its construction cloud Forma in March 2026 and owns Payapps (about US$381m, 2024). Oracle's Aconex holds correspondence and notices on many Tier 1 jobs. Procore is the one to meet directly: Datagrid's agents read ERPs and other systems, and the owner tools due in late 2026 give an owner a view of what the builder's Procore holds, as the builder configures it; they do not read Jobpac, PayApps or the inbox, and they report to Procore's customer. That is the reason the claim on this slide is bounded to "found none built for a lender's use" rather than an absolute; the defensible difference is lender-side reconciliation across systems, permissioning and reliance terms, and a condition in the facility, all of which are unproven today and are what the round tests. Tools beside: the lender's QS certifies monthly under AIQS guidance and records trade letting, variation claims and statutory declarations as reported; IPEX controls payments from a project bank account and says some lenders require it on certain jobs (vendor claim); Mastt is the owner's ledger; Built manages draws for 355 lenders in the US and Canada (vendor claim, October 2026). None of these is a partner by default; IRIS is used beside them, and a partnership would make sense with a product similar enough in logic to merge with, which we have not found. AI tools: Octant forecasts a builder's final cost and margin from its cost reports; Alloovium (Y Combinator, 2026) reads documents and email and drafts variations; Slate and Trunk Tools search a builder's records; Earlytrade sits on payables to offer trades early payment. Each reports to the party that pays. Recovery and restructuring advisers (insolvency practitioners, forensic QS, project advisers) rebuild a stalled job's position by hand over weeks; IRIS would feed that work, not replace it. The QS stays the lender's certifier; IRIS is an input to the QS and the lender, not a substitute.
