IRIS

The builder’s systems

Time, cost and quality risk for the lender.

Products in use in Australia unless marked. Procore, Aconex, Jobpac, PayApps and ProcurePro are systems IRIS reads. About sixty products and firms reviewed, November 2026; sources in appendix A8.

Products by what they read and who they serve. Lender's QS: progress drawdown reports certify work in place against the claim; reports state the builder's information was not independently verified. IPEX (Melbourne): a project payment account; certified progress reconciled against its own payment ledger at drawdown; vendor says some lenders mandate it; moves money, does not read the position. Korrix Intelligence Partners (Sydney): Assess, Monitor (monthly progress meetings, site walks, covenant register, risk brief, proposed as a loan condition) and Protect; a service, no software integrations. CreditorWatch and Equifax iCIRT: credit files and ratings on the builder company; nothing on the project. Cintech: one record per project from thirteen named connectors (Aconex, Procore, ProcurePro, PayApps, Jobpac and others), cited answers, AI workers, time bars; founders ex-CIO and ex-infrastructure lead at Tier 2 builders; no customers named. Vyne: decision intelligence for builders; illustrative preview. Slate Technologies (California): a decision assistant that ingests the major construction platforms, Procore named, and links to the schedule; Tier 1 general contractor customers; a real-estate intelligence module aimed at banks and REITs as land buyers; no lender project-position product; no Australian customers found. Octant AI (Brisbane; BMD-backed): machine-learning outturn forecasts from cost reports and schedules; names financiers among users of its insights but has no lender product. Mastt (Sydney): capital-project controls for owners and their PM consultants; no builder-system integration, no lender features. Procore with Datagrid: agents over the builder's ERP and documents, for the builder. Lendbase: a commercial-lending origination marketplace, upstream of the facility; a channel rather than a competitor. Not on the slide: Built, Rabbet and Land Gorilla (US lender draw tools, no Australian presence found); SteelCap (a landing page; removed 7 November 2026 at the owner's direction); Deep Space and Plexa (all-in-one builder platforms that replace the systems); Alloovium and Trunk Tools (builder-side AI); BuildGuard AI (CSIRO research stage). IPEX is not a partner. Full review: research/competitive-landscape.md.
