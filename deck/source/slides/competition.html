About sixty products and firms reviewed, November 2026; sources and detail in appendix A8.

Korrix Intelligence Partners (Sydney): Assess (builder financial health check from nine registers and reports), Monitor (monthly: progress meetings, site walks, covenant register, risk brief; proposed as a loan condition at a fixed monthly fee), Protect (insurance layer in development); reports only to the lender; no software integrations. SteelCap: a landing page, coming soon. IPEX: payment-account control with vendor-claimed lender mandates; moves money, does not read the position. Built: draw management for 300+ US lenders, reads Procore financials, checks the submitted package; no ANZ presence. Cintech: one record per project from thirteen named connectors (Aconex, Procore, ProcurePro, PayApps, Jobpac and others), cited answers, AI workers, time bars; founders ex-CIO and ex-infrastructure lead at Tier 2 builders; no customers named. Vyne: decision intelligence and organisational memory for builders; illustrative preview, no live systems. Procore with Datagrid: agents over the builder's ERP and documents, for the builder. Lendbase: a commercial-lending origination marketplace, upstream of the facility; a channel rather than a competitor. The lender's existing controls are the QS certificate, IPEX, project trust accounts and CreditorWatch or iCIRT ratings. IPEX is not a partner. Full review: research/competitive-landscape.md.
