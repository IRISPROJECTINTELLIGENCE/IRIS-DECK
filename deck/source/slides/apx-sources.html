A7

Backs the dated figures on slides 2, 3 and 8. Construction insolvencies: ASIC insolvency statistics, series 1, published 14 September 2026: 3,472 of 14,153 companies entering external administration in FY2025-26, 24.5%; the July and August 2026 share was 37.4% with the Bathla entities and 23.5% without. Bathla: 540 entities into administration on 25 August 2026 (ASIC data, via The Good Builder, 22 September); about A$3.3bn owed (AFR; others report $3.4bn); about 40 funds exposed (Bloomberg); redemptions paused from 14 August (ABC News, 28 August; ACI, 1 October); about $3.08bn owed to secured lenders, sites closed and receivers appointed (administrator’s updates, The Urban Developer, 25 September; ABC News, 17 October). Slide 3’s rating: the notes cite Rampart, 6 September 2026, and The Urban Developer, 25 August 2026. The project, the group’s administration and the earlier lender’s exit are confirmed in open sources; the 83% and the low-to-medium rating were not found in the open section of the article and are being confirmed. Rates and housing: RBA cash rate to 4.85% on 3 November 2026 after five rises from 3.60%; ABS monthly CPI, 4.9% to October, published 25 November; Cotality national index for October, published 2 November, 6.7% below the March peak. Regulation: ASIC REP 814, 820 and 823 (2025); the 52-fund review of $76bn before 30 June 2026; the Commissioner’s warning of 22 September; ASIC’s statement of 30 September naming residential construction. Funds: three listed credit funds suspended on 28 September after about $168M was written down, reinstated in October (ASX; CFS notice, 7 October); a $2.3bn secured loan fund suspended redemptions on 22 October (Financial Standard). Lending terms: Stamford Capital Real Estate Debt Capital Markets Survey 2026; Gravity Capital Australian Private Credit Index, September quarter 2026, published 25 October. Valuation: ASIC’s June 2026 notice on reassessing loan values, and the industry standard from 1 July 2027 (reported; the release number and standard reference are being added). Market and model inputs are on A5 and A6: IBISWorld E3019 (May 2026), E3021 (July 2026) and OD5428 (April 2026); ABS Building Activity, June 2026; CSQ; Hubexo Construction League, April 2026. Competitor sources are on A8. Founder observations: the replacement-builder premium of 20 to 40% and the three project examples on slide 5, which come from private records with figures altered so the jobs cannot be identified.
