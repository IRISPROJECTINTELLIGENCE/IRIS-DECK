A15

Backs slides 11 and 13. Each case re-runs the base model with the change named and everything else as on the Inputs sheet; the results are on the workbook’s Sensitivities sheet. Costs follow the plan as written, so hiring for lenders goes ahead in each case. In practice hiring after the seed waits for lenders, which is why the month cash would fall below zero is shown: it is the month by which that decision has to be made. Lenders twelve months later: the first lender at month 24 and 14 lenders by Year 5; Year 5 revenue $24.1M, EBITDA $7.1M, run rate $31.8M; cash stays positive with the seed at month 15. No lender in five years: builders and off-platform assessments reach $3.4M in Year 5; the plan’s hiring would take cash below zero at month 38, so hiring for lenders would stop before then. Lender retention: if each lender stops adding facilities three years after adopting, Year 5 revenue falls by $1.0M, because live facilities keep the condition to completion. The seed: the base case has $3M at month 15. A $1.5M seed is enough at the plan’s spend. At month 21, cash falls below zero from month 20, so the seed needs to land by month 19, or hiring after month 16 waits for it. With no seed at all, cash falls below zero from month 20 to month 27, at worst $79K, and recovers as lender revenue grows; that rests on lenders arriving on plan. Combined: lenders twelve months late and a $1.5M seed at month 21 take cash below zero from month 19, at worst $1.0M, so the plan would be re-cut before the seed. The single-input changes (facility size, fee, facilities per lender, loans re-signed, term, analyst ratio, director pay) are on the same sheet: each $10M of average facility is about $4.6M of Year 5 revenue, and each basis point of fee about $2.5M.
