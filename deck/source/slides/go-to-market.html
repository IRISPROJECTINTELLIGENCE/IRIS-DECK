Now · Builder pilots

Prove it on live jobs: two R&D deployments, then a monthly fee per project. Gate: the position reconciles to the builder’s own cost reports.

Builder · $3,500 a month per project

Alongside · Lender design partner

A non-bank lender shapes the lender view from the pilots and uses it on a live facility beside its QS. Gate: security, PI and reliance terms in place.

No fee · the partner’s time

Scale · The mandate

A credit committee accepts a live result and writes IRIS into one facility as a drawdown condition; then its book; then the next lender.

Developer · 15 bps a year on the facility, as it pays the QS

How the mandate holds

The facility binds the developer; the head contract and side deed bind the builder, from the next facility, extension or refinance. A builder under contract today owes nothing new.

Who opens the systems

The builder, read-only. It sees each finding first; builder, developer and lender each get their own view.

Nothing assumed automatic: no access, no pilot; no reconciled result, no lender test; no live result, no condition. The legal pathway is confirmed in this round (slide 14).

Three workstreams on one platform, in sequence (D-07): builder pilots are the technical and commercial proving ground and a revenue line of their own; the lender design partner (target: a non-bank private credit fund) shapes the lender view from those pilots with the pilot builder's consent; at scale the lender requires IRIS as a drawdown condition, the developer pays in basis points as it pays the QS, and the builder opens its systems under the head contract and side deed. Distressed-project assessments are part of the scale stage, priced per engagement. Mandate mechanics: lender to developer by the facility agreement; developer to all bidders by a tender or award condition; developer to builder by a head-contract clause; all three parties by the side deed at financial close. A builder that carves it out gets IRIS's view of its own job; we expect a carve-out to read as a signal to the lender, which is untested until a lender adopts. Each figure links to its record, so a disputed finding is checked against data the builder does not write. Appendix A4 carries the mandate table.
