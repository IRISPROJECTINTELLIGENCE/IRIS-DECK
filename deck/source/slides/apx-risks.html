A10

Backs the milestones on slide 13. Each question is one an investor would ask before the seed, and the round is built to answer them in this order. 1. Live data, month 6: the first release of the platform plan (pilot proof) running on the R&D partner’s jobs and judged against the pass marks on A14. The R&D partnership is agreed; its agreement and data terms are being finalised. 2. Paying builders, month 6: the model has two builders paying from month 6; the R&D partner uses IRIS without a fee. 3. Security, month 6: an independent security audit and penetration test, with the access review and the questionnaire pack lenders send; certification to a standard follows when a lender’s procurement requires it (A13). 4. A lender using it, month 9: the design partner runs a scoped lender view beside its QS on one live facility. The platform plan places full lender and developer views in its seventh release, so month 9 depends on a scoped view being brought forward on the first release’s data and access controls; to be confirmed with the technical lead. 5. The documents, month 9: a legal opinion on the drawdown condition, the tender condition, the head contract clause and the side deed (A4). 6. The condition, month 12: the first drawdown condition with the design partner, a company target. 7. Fundability, months 15 to 18: the seed is raised on paid builder use, with or without the condition; at the plan’s spend it is needed by month 19 (A15). Other risks. Competition: platforms report to their own customer, and requiring one would dictate the builder’s software; QS and restructuring firms could add the capability and are as likely to be channels as rivals; Procore with Datagrid is the platform to watch (slide 9). Sales cycles: lender procurement, panels and legal review are slow; lenders a year late take Year 5 revenue to $24M with cash still positive (A15). Connections: builder-authorised API connections, one adapter per system, built system by system, Procore first. Assessments: access after an appointment, and pricing, which is a fixed fee so that IRIS has no stake in the recovery (A9).
