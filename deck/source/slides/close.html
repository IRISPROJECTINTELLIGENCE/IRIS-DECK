A risk tool for development

A funded project’s position moves week to week and reaches its lender late, in part, by hand. IRIS reconciles it from the project’s own records, source-linked, for builder, developer and lender.

Builders prove it on live jobs while a lender design partner shapes the view. At scale it is written into the facility and paid in basis points. The $500K funds the evidence: live data, reconciliation to builders’ own cost reports, a lender live beside its QS, security and professional review.
