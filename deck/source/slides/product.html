IRIS

One view across the systems, each finding linked to its record, and a chat that answers from the records.

Position

Where the project stands across cost, time, scope, procurement, payment, quality, safety and compliance, checked against the baseline.

Exposure

Unpriced variations, delays without notice, trades let against allowances, time bars, open defects, stop-works, approval and insurance gaps; each valued and dated.

Gaps and conflicts

What should exist and doesn’t, and where two records disagree: the notice not lodged, the variation not priced, the register that doesn’t match the ledger.

Three states, kept separate on purpose. Built: the MVP runs on a populated Procore sandbox and produces a position across cost, time and scope with drill-down and a chat that answers from the records; it has not been connected to a live project. In build with this round, the enterprise product at V1: the adapter layer (one adapter per system API, a systems register per project so each job declares what it runs), the production platform with role-based views for the builder, the developer and the lender, each seeing the project's risk position and the delivery-side actions that close or mitigate each risk, proven on live records. V1 is built to be used by a lender on a project; the lender design partner shapes how that view is structured. Next rounds: the portfolio view across a lender's book, which needs a mandate to shape; the distressed-assessment workflow on slide 10, which needs a distressed deployment to shape; further domains and functions. Proof on live records means the position reconciles to the builder's own monthly cost report and, for the three historic record sets, to the outcome reached; the deck states no accuracy percentage, protocol or tolerance, at the owner's direction. Universal API coverage is not claimed: coverage is built system by system, and a system without an adapter is declared on the register as outside the position until one exists. IRIS does not replace QS site inspections, an independent cost-to-complete assessment or professional judgement; it reconciles the records those rely on and shows where they disagree. Deterministic rules over the canonical record; each figure links to its source; a chat answers from the same records with the source shown.
