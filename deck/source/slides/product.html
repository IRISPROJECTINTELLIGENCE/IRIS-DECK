The product

05

IRIS reads the builder’s systems and shows the lender
the position beneath the certificate.

How it connects

Read-only connections, authorised by the builder, to Procore or Aconex, Jobpac, PayApps, ProcurePro and the inbox.

Nothing is installed. Nothing is written back. The QS and the site visit stay.

What the lender sees, continuously

Claimed, certified, paid and owed to the trades, side by side.

Forecast final cost against the ledger, and how much of it is still contingency.

Notices, variations and delays, dated against their time bars.

Each figure linked to the record it came from.

The monthly certificate reports a summary. IRIS reports the records. It values nothing and certifies nothing; it shows the lender what the records say, and where two records disagree.

What IRIS produces from the reconciled records: the position (where the project stands on cost, time, scope, procurement, payment, quality, safety and compliance, against the baseline); the exposure (unpriced variations, delays without notice, trades let against allowances, open defects, approval and insurance gaps, each valued and dated); and gaps and conflicts (what should exist and doesn't, and where two records disagree). IRIS reports the system of record and flags the gap where the builder's internal and client-facing forecasts differ; it does not adjudicate. It does not replace the QS, the site inspection, an independent cost-to-complete assessment or professional judgement, and makes no builder scoring, company-wide solvency assessment or payment surveillance across a builder's book. Built: MVP on a populated Procore sandbox. This round: enterprise V1, an adapter per source system, lender, builder and developer views, proven on live records.
