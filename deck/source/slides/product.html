Built: MVP on a Procore sandbox. This round: enterprise V1, an adapter per system, three views, proven on live records. Next: portfolio view across a lender’s book; assessments.

What IRIS produces from the reconciled records: the position (where the project stands on cost, time, scope, procurement, payment, quality, safety and compliance, against the baseline); the exposure (unpriced variations, delays without notice, trades let against allowances, open defects, approval and insurance gaps, each valued and dated); and gaps and conflicts (what should exist and doesn't, and where two records disagree). Each party gets its own view of the same position; commercially sensitive items stay with the builder. IRIS values nothing and certifies nothing; it reports the system of record and flags the gap where the builder's internal and client-facing forecasts differ. Distressed-project assessment is proposed functionality at a separate fee, offered after the platform is proven on live jobs; no engagement has been run. Before an appointment, access runs through the facility's information rights and the builder's side deed; after, through the receiver's or administrator's agreement. The six-step assessment workflow and the lender decisions the position serves are in the appendix (A1, A9, A12).
