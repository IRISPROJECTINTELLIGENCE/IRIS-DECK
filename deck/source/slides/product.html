IRIS

Reconciliation and risk intelligence: one view across the systems, drill-down to the record, and a chat that answers from it.

Position

Where the job stands in each part of delivery: contract, programme, cost and cash flow, procurement, design, quality, safety, compliance.

Exposure

Unpriced variations, delays without notice, trades let against allowances, time bars, open defects, stop-works, approval and insurance gaps; each valued and dated.

Gaps and conflicts

What should exist and doesn’t, and where two records disagree: the notice not lodged, the variation not priced, the register that doesn’t match the ledger.

IRIS for Builders

The same view, first: notices near time bars, unlet scope, trades owed, defects. The builder keeps its margin. $3,500 a month per live job.

IRIS for Lenders

Time, cost and quality on each facility, continuously, down to the record. In the facility; the developer pays 15 bps a year.

Permissions and distress

The builder authorises and sees each finding first; lender and developer see the reconciled view. In distress, the full picture in days.

Three states, kept separate on purpose. Built: the proof of concept runs on a populated Procore sandbox and produces a position across cost, time and scope with drill-down and a chat that answers from the records; it has not been connected to a live project. In build with this round, the enterprise product at V1: the adapter layer (one adapter per system API, a systems register per project so each job declares what it runs), the production platform with role-based views for the builder, the developer and the lender, each seeing the project's risk position and the delivery-side actions that close or mitigate each risk, proven on live records. V1 is built to be used by a lender on a project; the lender design partner shapes how that view is structured. Next rounds: the portfolio view across a lender's book, which needs a mandate to shape; the distressed-assessment workflow on slide 10, which needs a distressed deployment to shape; further domains and functions. Proof on live records means the position reconciles to the builder's own monthly cost report and, for the three historic record sets, to the outcome reached; the deck states no accuracy percentage, protocol or tolerance, at the owner's direction. Universal API coverage is not claimed: coverage is built system by system, and a system without an adapter is declared on the register as outside the position until one exists. IRIS does not replace QS site inspections, an independent cost-to-complete assessment or professional judgement; it reconciles the records those rely on and shows where they disagree. Deterministic rules over the canonical record; each figure links to its source; a chat answers from the same records with the source shown. One platform, two products (D-35): the builder's product and the lender's product read the same reconciled position; what differs is the view, the entitlement and the buyer. The developer's fee is paid out of the facility; the hypothesis that a monitored facility prices inside an unmonitored one is tested with the design partner at month 9.
