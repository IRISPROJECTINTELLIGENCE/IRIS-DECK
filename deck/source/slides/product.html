The developer line is a hypothesis: that lenders price monitored delivery inside unmonitored delivery. It is tested with the design partner at month 9.

What IRIS produces from the reconciled records: the position (where the project stands on cost, time, scope, procurement, payment, quality, safety and compliance, against the baseline); the exposure (unpriced variations, delays without notice, trades let against allowances, open defects, approval and insurance gaps, each valued and dated); and gaps and conflicts (what should exist and doesn't, and where two records disagree). Each party gets its own view of the same position; commercially sensitive items stay with the builder. IRIS values nothing and certifies nothing; it reports the system of record and flags the gap where the builder's internal and client-facing forecasts differ. Developer financing cost (D-33, hypothesis): if a lender prices a monitored facility 25–50 bps inside an unmonitored one, a 15 bps fee leaves the developer ahead; no lender has yet said so, and the design partner is asked at month 9. Distressed-project assessment is proposed functionality at a separate fee, offered after the platform is proven on live jobs; no engagement has been run; the workflow is appendix A12 and the lender decisions the position serves are A9. Built: MVP on a Procore sandbox. This round: enterprise V1, an adapter per source system, builder, developer and lender views, proven on live records.
