Why now

08

Lenders now need to see inside the project.
Capital is still being deployed. It is asking harder questions.

The reason · 2026

Rates and feasibility. The cash rate rose 125 bps in 2026 to 4.85%; CPI 4.9% to October. Dwelling values 6.7% below their March peak (RBA; ABS; Cotality).

Bathla. 540 entities into administration in August, owing about $3.3bn to about 40 funds. Sites closed; receivers in by September (reported).

Valuation scrutiny. ASIC reviewed 52 funds holding $76bn, warned them to prepare for enforcement, and named residential construction (ASIC; ASX; reported).

Not just one group. A $2.3bn credit fund froze redemptions in October. Construction was 37% of insolvencies in July–August, 23.5% without Bathla (ASIC; reported).

The enabler · 2025

AI now reads contracts, emails and cost records together and cites the source of each figure, at a cost that works per project.

The market’s answer so far

Lending has tightened since Bathla. Lenders now want presales before full leverage, favour experienced sponsors and have largely stopped funding thin-equity deals (lender survey; private credit index). All of it is decided before the first drawdown.

From July 2027 fund valuations need independent challenge, quarterly as good practice; ASIC says not to wait for a default (FSC Standard 30; ASIC).
