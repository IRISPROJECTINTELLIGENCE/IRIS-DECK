08

A lender-side category is forming.
None of it reads the builder’s systems.

Failure

Bathla: 540 entities into administration in August 2026, about $3.3bn owed to about 40 funds; sites closed and receivers appointed within a month (reported).

Regulators

ASIC reviewed 52 private credit funds holding $76bn, warned of enforcement on 22 September, and named residential construction on 30 September (reported).

Rates

The cash rate rose 100 bps in 2026 to 4.60%; CPI 4.3% to September; dwelling values 5.2% below their March peak (RBA; ABS; Cotality).

The market’s answer

Lender-side monitoring is being sold as a loan condition, done with site walks and register checks. Builder-side AI is being sold to builders, on builders’ data. The builder’s own records, read for the lender, are open ground.

Dated facts, October–November 2026. Bathla: 540 entities into administration on 25 August; about $3.3bn owed (AFR total; $3.08bn to secured lenders per The Urban Developer, 25 September); 13 remaining sites closed on 23 September; receivers appointed on individual projects (ABC, 17 October).
