Why now

08

The market moved to where the position matters most.
Capital is still being deployed. It is asking harder questions.

Rates

The cash rate rose 100 bps in 2026 to 4.60%; CPI 4.3% to September; dwelling values 5.2% below their March peak (RBA; ABS; Cotality).

Failure

Bathla: 540 entities into administration in August, about $3.3bn owed to about 40 funds; receivers appointed by late September (reported).

Regulators

ASIC reviewed 52 private credit funds holding $76bn, warned of enforcement on 22 September, and named residential construction on 30 September (reported).

Insolvencies

Construction was about one in four Australian insolvencies in FY2025–26, and 37% in July–August with Bathla (ASIC).
