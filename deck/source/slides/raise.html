Milestones are framed as evidence created, not months elapsed, so the round has a purpose even if the first lender condition slips: by month 12 the company should hold live API access on real jobs, a signed comparison against builders' own cost reports and the three historic record sets, a lender design partner using the lender view on a live facility beside its QS, security certification, professional indemnity and reliance terms, and paid builder projects; the distressed-project assessment offer is scoped, not sold, inside the round. Each milestone names the artefact it leaves, so progress can be checked against documents rather than statements: signed pilot agreements and the design partner's letter (months 1–3); the comparison in two parts, first against the three historic record sets, then against the pilot builders' own cost reports with each builder signing it (months 4 and 6); the security certificate under the standard the owner selects; the design partner's written review of the lender view beside its QS report (months 6–9); the PI policy, reliance terms and legal opinion (month 9); paid invoices, a drafted drawdown condition with one lender and a scoped assessment offer with a fee (month 12).
