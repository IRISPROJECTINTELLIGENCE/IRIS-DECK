The round is framed as a build. $750K on a SAFE at a $4M cap (owner decision, 24 November 2026; previously $500K at $5M) takes IRIS from the proof of concept on slide 7 to an enterprise product: built for production with an external engineering partner, security-audited and running on live projects with the R&D partner builder and a design-partner lender. Use of funds sums to $750K: $320K enterprise platform build (contract engineering), founders at $100K each plus 12% super ($224K), $46K security audit, cloud and models ($30K and $16K), $20K sales, $20K legal and IP, $20K contingency including $10K insurance, and a $100K runway reserve held to the seed. The split follows the owner's direction of 24 November: $150K of the increase to the build, $100K to runway. The build line is an allowance until the engineering partner has quoted against the statement of work; say so if asked. Year 1 costs in the workbook are $669K against $650K of spend lines here; Year 1 revenue of $187K covers the difference and the reserve stays in cash. Milestones name what exists at each point: live jobs connected through the R&D partnership and a design-partner lender engaged (months 1–3); the enterprise product reconciling to the builders' own cost reports, a passed security audit under the standard the owner selects, and a first paying builder (month 6); IRIS for Lenders running on a live facility beside the lender's QS, with PI cover, reliance terms and a legal opinion (month 9); builders paying across their jobs, a drawdown condition drafted with one lender and a scoped assessment offer with a fee (month 12). The first drawdown condition at month 12 is the base-case assumption in the model and a company target, not a commitment; the seed in months 15–18 is raised on a product in paid use, with the mandate as upside. The R&D partnership on slide 2 is the first builder; the introduction wanted is a second. Cash: with no revenue the round funds about 13 months of the Year 1 plan; the base case reaches the seed on Year 1 builder revenue of $187K from paying builders other than the R&D partner, none of whom is signed yet.
