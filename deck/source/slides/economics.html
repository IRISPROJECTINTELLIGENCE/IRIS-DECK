All figures from financial-model/IRIS_model_36m.xlsx, base case (D-33, 1 November 2026). Builder line: two pilot builders paid from month 6, builders 3, 4 and 5 from months 12, 20 and 28; each starts with two live jobs on IRIS and adds about one every two months to a cap of six; $3,500 a month per job. Facility line: lenders adopting at months 12, 18, 22, 26, 29, 32, 34 and 36, each adding 0.8 new facilities a month (about ten a year, roughly $770M of originations, a $1.5bn construction book on 24-month terms) on a 24-month term; $80M average facility across the project debt stack at 15 bps, so the $10,000 a month cap binds at $120,000 a year. Costs: founders at $100K each plus 12% super; hires from month 16 (commercial lead, engineer, deployment analyst), a lender onboarding lead at 18, head of product and risk and customer success at 22, further engineers and analysts to 15 people at month 36; platform build $170K in Year 1 then contract engineering $90K a year; cloud $800 a month fixed plus $150 per live job; security certification $30K then $35K a year; insurance $10K / $45K / $90K; sales $20K / $120K / $300K; legal $20K / $40K / $90K; contingency $10K then 5% of costs. Year 1 costs equal the $500K use of funds. Cash before the seed stays positive throughout, with a low of $177K at month 11; the $3M seed at month 15 funds the hires that precede the lender revenue. Conservative case (the previous base, D-07): four lenders at 0.5 facilities a month on $60M, five builders to five jobs each from month 7, nine people; revenue $122K / $847K / $2,157K; run rate $3.0M.
