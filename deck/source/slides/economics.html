All figures from financial-model/IRIS_model_5y.xlsx, base case (D-37, 4 November 2026; the developer-direct line was removed on 26 November 2026 at the owner's decision, D-48, so the model carries the two products and assessments). Market: IBISWorld building work in scope converted to value under construction by build period, divided by a $40M average contract for the market (D-52); facility fee 15 bps on the project debt stack at 1.2 times the build contract, above a $3,500 a month floor, with no cap (D-51); assessments at 4% of funded projects a year × a $35,000 fee benchmarked to QS reports. Lender line: lenders adopting at months 12, 18, 22, 26, 29, 32, 34, 36 and then about every two months to 20 at month 60; each adds 0.8 new facilities a month and ten facilities from its existing book over 18 months as loans re-paper; 24-month term; the facilities IRIS is written into average $80M across the whole debt stack (senior alone or senior with mezzanine), entered directly by case, which is $120,000 a year at 15 bps. One average flexed by case is the owner's direction of 26 November 2026 (D-49); the $80M base is his direction of the same day (D-50), replacing $48M. Builder line: two pilots paid from month 6, then builders at months 12, 20, 28, 33, 37, 40, 43, 46, 48, 50, 52, 54, 56, 58, 60; each starts with two jobs and grows to six; $3,500 a month per job. Assessments from month 30: 60% of on-platform distressed facilities, plus off-platform wins ramping to eight a year. Costs: founders at $100K then $180K from Year 3; fixed roles by start month; deployment analysts one per eight live jobs; a lender success manager per two lenders; two engineers plus one per 1.5 lenders; commissions 8% of first-year fees on new facilities; $4,000 to connect each facility; cloud $250 a month per live job; PI insurance to $500K; sales and marketing to $1.6M; 5% contingency. Year 1 costs ($669K) track the $650K of spend lines in the $750K use of funds; the $100K runway reserve stays in cash. Upside: lenders faster, 1.0 facilities a month, 14 from the book, and a $102M facility; conservative: seven lenders at 0.5 a month, a $48M facility at 12.5 bps, overheads at half. Seed $3M at month 15 in all cases; Series A optional and shown separately.
