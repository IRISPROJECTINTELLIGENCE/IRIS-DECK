Builder line: two pilot builders paid from month 6, builders 3–5 from months 12, 20 and 28; each starts with two live jobs at $3,500 a month and adds about one every two months to a cap of six. Lender line: lenders adopting at months 12, 18, 22, 26, 29, 32, 34 and 36, each adding 0.8 new facilities a month on a 24-month term; $80M average facility across the debt stack, so the cap binds at $120,000 a year. Costs: founders at $100K plus super; hires from month 16 to 15 people at month 36; platform, cloud, security, insurance, sales and legal as listed on the Inputs sheet.
