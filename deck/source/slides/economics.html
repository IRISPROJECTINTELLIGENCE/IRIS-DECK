What drives the curve. One lender adopting brings its book: each lender adds about 1.5 facilities a month at 15 bps on a $35M facility ($52.5K a year), and builders under the condition bring their other jobs. Year 3 recognises $5.8M; the month-36 run rate is $8.9M.
