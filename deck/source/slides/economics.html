A 36-month driver model, in the workbook. Two revenue lines: builder subscriptions at $3,500 a month per project, from month 6 after the R&D deployments reconcile to the builders' own cost reports; and facility fees once a lender makes IRIS a condition, the first at month 12, paid by the developer at 15 bps on an average $35M facility, so about $52,500 a year each. Lenders 2, 3 and 4 follow at months 21, 27 and 33, each after a live result; each adds about $530K a year of fees by month 36. Costs: Year 1 is the $500K use of funds on slide 17 (founders at $100K each plus super, $170K contract engineering); hires start at month 16, after the seed round; cloud and model costs scale with live jobs; insurance rises with lender reliance; contingency is 5% of costs from Year 2. The conservative case halves the builder adds, delays the first facility to month 18, prices at 12.5 bps on $30M and hires later. No recovery or assessment revenue, no insurers, no R&D tax refund. Reconciliation of the two headline figures: Year 3 recognised revenue is $2,615K; the month-36 run rate is $3,626K in the workbook, being 35.7 paid projects at $42,000 a year plus 40.5 facilities at $52,500 a year (the slide shows the counts rounded to 36 and 41, which multiply to $3.66M); the difference between the two headline figures is growth through the year, not a different model. The facility-fee line, 53% of Year 3 revenue, is a scenario that depends on a first lender condition at month 12 and three more lenders following; no lender has agreed to anything today, and the builder line alone is the conservative case. The model depends on the seed round arriving in month 15 (base) or 18 (conservative) and on the first lender condition at month 12 or 18; both are assumptions, not commitments.
