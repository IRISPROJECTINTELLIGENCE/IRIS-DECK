Two revenue lines: builder subscriptions at $3,500 a month per project, from month 6 after the R&D deployments reconcile to the builders' own cost reports; and facility fees once a lender makes IRIS a condition, the first at month 12, paid by the developer at 15 bps on an average $35M facility, so about $52,500 a year each. Lenders 2, 3 and 4 follow at months 21, 27 and 33, each after a live result; each adds about $530K a year of fees by month 36. Costs: Year 1 is the use of funds on slide 17; hires start at month 16, after the seed round; cloud and model costs scale with live jobs; insurance rises with lender reliance; contingency is 5% of costs from Year 2. The conservative case halves the builder adds, delays the first facility to month 18, prices at 12.5 bps on $30M and hires later. No recovery or assessment revenue, no insurers, no R&D tax refund. Reconciliation of the two headline figures: Year 3 recognised revenue is $2,615K; the month-36 run rate is about $3.66M, being 36 paid projects at $42,000 a year plus 41 facilities at $52,500 a year; the difference is growth through the year, not a different model. The model depends on the seed round arriving in month 15 (base) or 18 (conservative) and on the first lender condition at month 12 or 18; both are assumptions, not commitments.
