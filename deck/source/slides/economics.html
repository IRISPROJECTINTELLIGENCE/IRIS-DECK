Market: IBISWorld building work in scope converted to value under construction by build period, divided by a $40M average contract; facility fee 15 bps on the project debt stack at 1.2 times the build contract, within a $3,500 floor and $10,000 cap a month; assessments at 4% of funded projects a year × a $35,000 fee benchmarked to QS reports. Lender line: lenders adopting at months 12, 18, 22, 26, 29, 32, 34, 36 and then about every two months to 20 at month 60; each adds 0.8 new facilities a month and ten facilities from its existing book over 18 months as loans re-paper; 24-month term; $80M average facility, so the cap binds at $120,000 a year. Builder line: two pilots paid from month 6, then builders at months 12, 20, 28, 33, 37, 40, 43, 46, 48, 50, 52, 54, 56, 58, 60; each starts with two jobs and grows to six; $3,500 a month per job. Developer-direct: 0.5 projects a month from month 30 at $2,500. Assessments from month 30: 60% of on-platform distressed facilities, plus off-platform wins ramping to eight a year. Costs: founders at $100K then $180K from Year 3; fixed roles by start month; deployment analysts one per eight live jobs; a lender success manager per two lenders; two engineers plus one per 1.5 lenders; commissions 8% of first-year fees on new facilities; $4,000 to connect each facility; cloud $250 a month per live job; PI insurance to $500K; sales and marketing to $1.6M; 5% contingency. Year 1 costs about equal the $500K use of funds. Upside: lenders faster, 1.0 facilities a month, 14 from the book; conservative: seven lenders at 0.5 a month, $60M facilities at 12.5 bps, overheads at half. Seed $3M at month 15 in all cases; Series A optional and shown separately.
