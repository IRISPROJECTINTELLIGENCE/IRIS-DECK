When a funded project stalls, the first job is to find its position.
IRIS is built to do that from the records, not from each party’s version.

Proposed functionality, at a separate fee, once the platform is proven on live jobs. No engagement has been run.

1 · Trigger

A missed in-balance test, a builder default or administration, a receiver appointed, a refinance or restructure under review.

2 · Buyer

The lender, its receiver or administrator, or the capital partner. Paid per engagement, with the other recovery costs.

3 · Access, lawfully

Facility information rights and the builder’s side deed; a receiver’s or administrator’s powers; or the developer’s consent.

4 · Ingest

The job’s systems register: cost ledger, commitments, claims, variations, notices, programme, correspondence; the QS pack beside them.

5 · Outputs

Cost to complete, committed and owed, unlet scope, disputed variations, delay entitlement, programme exposure; a register of gaps.

6 · Decisions

Complete with the builder, replace it, refinance, restructure, sell or enforce; what a replacement builder’s premium is pricing.

Today the cost is weeks of record rebuild while the site stalls, interest runs and buyers near sunset dates. The value is a complete, source-linked position sooner, with a trail a committee, a court or a buyer can check. Gaps are listed, not filled in.

Sequence: builder pilots prove the position, a lender design partner shapes the view, monitoring scales it; assessments follow, priced per engagement. Before distress, the same position serves routine lender decisions (A2).

09

Proposed, not demonstrated, and deliberately last in the sequence: the platform is proven on builder pilots and shaped with a lender design partner first, then written into facilities at scale; distressed-project assessments are offered after that, at a separate per-engagement fee, because they are where the information problem is most expensive and where a lender already pays outside advisers, so a budget exists. The first assessment is a controlled one on a historic record set, scored against the outcome reached, before any live engagement. Access: on a live job before failure, the facility agreement's information undertakings and the builder's side deed carry access; after an appointment, the administrator or receiver controls the company's books and the builder's software subscriptions must be kept alive, so access needs their agreement and is not automatic; the developer's own records and the lender's QS pack are available regardless. What the recovery assessment does not do: value the asset, certify cost to complete, or assess the builder's solvency; the QS, the valuer and the insolvency practitioner stay. Speed: the design aim is a first position in days from access, not hours, and the time depends on how complete and well-kept the records are; where they are thin, the output is a register of gaps rather than a filled-in number. Pricing is a hypothesis: a fixed fee per engagement, not a share of recovery, to avoid a conflict between the position reported and the fee earned. No engagement, fee, volume or conversion is modelled on slide 15.
