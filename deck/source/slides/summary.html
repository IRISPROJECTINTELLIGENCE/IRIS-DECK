Simply put.

The problem

When a funded project gets into trouble, the first question is where it really stands. The answer sits in five systems, is assembled by hand each month, and reaches the people funding the job late and filtered.

The product

IRIS reads those systems, read-only, and reconciles them into one risk position. Each finding links to its record.

Who it serves

Lenders see the projects they fund. Builders catch risk early and keep their margin. Developers finance on clearer terms. In distress, a position in days.

The wedge

Builders subscribe first, by live job. Lender mandates scale it.

Where we are

MVP on a Procore sandbox; live-project access being negotiated. Pre-revenue.
