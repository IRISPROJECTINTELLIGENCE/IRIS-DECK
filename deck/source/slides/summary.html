Simply put.

The problem

Construction is about one in four Australian insolvencies (ASIC, FY2025–26). Lenders fund on the cost to finish. The real figure sits in the builder’s systems, pieced together by hand each month, and reaches the lender late, incomplete and filtered.

The product

IRIS reads the builder’s systems, read-only, and shows where the job stands on time, cost and quality, and what puts each at risk. Each finding links to its record. IRIS works beside the Quantity Surveyor.

Two products, one platform

IRIS for Builders, now: a monthly fee per live job. It proves the product and protects the builder’s margin.
IRIS for Lenders, alongside: a design-partner lender shapes it. At scale it becomes a condition of the loan, paid by the developer. Distressed-project assessments for a separate fee.

Why now

Rates up 125 bps in 2026. A $3.3bn developer-builder failure in August hit about 40 credit funds; redemptions froze. ASIC is moving to enforcement, and lenders are asking harder questions. IRIS gives lenders the clarity into delivery risk they need to protect their funds.

Where we are

Proof of concept running on a Procore sandbox. R&D partnership secured with a South East Queensland Tier 2 builder, with real project data. Technical build partner in negotiation. Validated by builders, including a regional commercial manager at a multinational Tier 1. A private lender has confirmed the product's value proposition. Pre-revenue.

The round

$750K SAFE at a $4M post-money cap. It takes IRIS from proof of concept to a security-audited enterprise product, live with our builder partner and a lender.
