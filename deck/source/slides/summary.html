Simply put.

The problem

Construction is about one in four Australian insolvencies (ASIC, FY2025–26). When a funded project gets into trouble, the first question is where it really stands. That answer sits in five systems, is assembled by hand monthly, and reaches the funders late and filtered.

The product

IRIS reads those systems by read-only API and reconciles them into one risk position, by domain, each finding linked to its record. It writes nothing back. The QS stays.

Who pays

Builders, monthly per project, now. Developers, in basis points on the facility, once a lender writes IRIS into it. That mandate is the scale route: one lender brings its book.

Why now

Rates up 100 bps in 2026; a $3.3bn developer-builder failure in August; ASIC moving on private credit valuations. Capital still flows, on harder questions (reported).

Where we are

MVP on a populated Procore sandbox, shown to a South East Queensland Tier 2 builder; live-project access being negotiated. Pre-revenue.

The round

$500K SAFE at a $5M cap. Buys live data, reconciliation to builders’ own cost reports, a lender beside its QS, a security review and paid demand.
