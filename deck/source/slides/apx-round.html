A16

Backs slide 13. Instrument: a simple agreement for future equity (SAFE) for A$750K, converting at the next priced round at a valuation cap of A$4M (owner decision, 24 November 2026, D-39; previously $500K at $5M). Whether the cap is pre- or post-money, any discount, pro rata rights and a most-favoured-nation clause are set in the SAFE itself and are to be confirmed by the company. Use of funds: $320K for the enterprise product build with an engineering partner, an allowance until the partner’s quote against the statement of work; $224K for the two directors at $100K each plus 12% superannuation, full-time from close; $46K for the security audit, cloud and models; $20K sales; $20K legal and IP; $20K contingency and insurance; and a $100K runway reserve held to the seed. Runway: the round covers 13 months of the plan’s costs with no revenue; with the plan’s revenue, cash stays above $261K before the seed. The seed: $3M is assumed at month 15, raised on paid builder use with or without a lender’s condition; at the plan’s spend it needs to land by month 19, or hiring after month 16 waits (A15). What the round retires: whether the reconciliation holds on live data, whether builders pay, whether a lender’s security review passes, whether a lender uses the view beside its QS, and whether the documents hold (A10). Month 9 depends on a scoped lender view being brought forward from the seventh release of the platform plan (A12).
