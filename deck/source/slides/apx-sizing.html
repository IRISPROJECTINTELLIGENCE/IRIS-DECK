A5

Backs the three market figures on slide 11. Each step is a line on the workbook’s Market sheet, and each input is on the Inputs sheet with its source and type, so the chain can be rebuilt from the published figures. 1. Building work a year in scope: apartment work, medium-rise $18.9bn plus high-rise $14.6bn (IBISWorld E3019, 2025-26), plus commercial and industrial building of $70.9bn (IBISWorld E3021, 2026-27) × 65% on jobs of $10M or more, a company estimate because IBISWorld does not split by job size: $33.5bn + $46.1bn = $79.6bn. 2. Value under construction at one time = work a year × build time: apartments 29 months (ABS average completion time, 2025-26), commercial 24 months (estimate; ABS non-residential work yet to be done was $76.4bn in March 2026 against about $70bn a year): $81.0bn + $92.2bn = $173.1bn. 3. With a construction facility: developers fund 88% of apartment and 73.1% of commercial work (IBISWorld major markets): $138.6bn. 4. Live jobs = value ÷ a $40M market average contract: 4,328, of which 3,465 carry a facility and 863 do not. Cross-checks on the $40M: a listed lender’s senior construction loans average about $39M (June 2026); Brisbane and Gold Coast apartment jobs on hold average about $48M (PRD, September 2026); the top 50 builders’ 2025 starts averaged about $61M (Hubexo, April 2026). 5. TAM: each live job is priced once. A job with a facility pays 15 bps on the market’s average facility, $40M × 1.2 = $48M, which is $72,000 a year; a job without one pays the builder subscription, $3,500 a month or $42,000 a year; assessments add 4% of funded jobs a year × $35,000 = $4.9M. TAM = 3,465 × $72,000 + 863 × $42,000 + $4.9M = $290.6M. 6. SAM: the jobs with a construction facility, where a lender can set the condition: $249.5M of facility fees plus $4.9M of assessments = $254.4M (D-54). It leaves out the $36.2M of subscriptions on jobs with no facility, which the plan reaches builder by builder. Queensland, where the first builders are, is the first market: 816 live jobs and $54.9M priced the same way; South East Queensland is about 79% of the state’s pipeline (CSQ). 7. SOM: the base case’s run rate at month 60, 373 facilities × $120,000 + 92 builder jobs × $42,000 + assessments = $49.2M, 19% of SAM and 17% of TAM. SOM is counted the same way as TAM, live jobs at one moment × their annual price, which is why it uses the month-60 run rate; Year 5 revenue, the sum of months 49 to 60, is $40.5M (A6). Checks: the 465 jobs under IRIS at month 60 are 11% of live jobs; the 373 facilities at $80M are $29.8bn, 18% of the $166bn of construction facility value ($138.6bn × 1.2). Three sizes: $40M counts the jobs in the market; $48M is the market’s average facility and prices the market; $80M is the average facility the plan’s lenders put under IRIS (D-50), larger because the lenders that adopt first write the mid- and high-rise jobs. It sets the lender revenue line and leaves the market unchanged (D-52). Out of scope: low-rise apartments, townhouses and houses, mostly jobs under $10M, and public work, which carries no construction facility. Insurers and surety providers are not counted. The $40M average and the 65% share move the market most; both are estimates.
