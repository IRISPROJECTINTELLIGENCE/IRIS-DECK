A4

Each step is a line on the Market sheet of the workbook, and each input is on the Inputs sheet with its source and type. Measured or reported: the IBISWorld segment revenues, major-market shares and state shares; the ABS apartment completion time. Estimates: the 65% share of commercial work on jobs of $10M or more, the 24-month commercial build period, the $40M average job and the facility-to-contract ratio. Cross-checks on job size: the top 50 builders started 722 projects worth $43.9bn in 2025, about $61M each (Hubexo); Brisbane and Gold Coast apartment projects on hold average about $48M (PRD, September 2026); Qualitas senior construction loans average about $39M (June 2026). Out of scope: low-rise apartments, townhouses and house building, which are mostly jobs under $10M, and public work, which carries no construction facility. Insurers and surety providers: about 5% of contract value sits as security on live jobs; at 0.5–2% a year that is about $87M of fees, none of it counted.
