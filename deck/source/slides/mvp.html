The proof of concept today: one project, six domains.

Demonstration build on a Procore sandbox; the project and figures are fictional. Real project data follows through our Tier 2 R&D partner.

The screen is the proof of concept as it runs today on a populated Procore sandbox: a fictional project with a $142.5M head contract and a forecast of $148.4M, reporting period May 2026. Top row: the project position, with value at risk ($5.9M of forecast head-contract value), time at risk (practical completion forecast to slip eight weeks) and cash lag ($12.9M of cost incurred and not yet recovered), plus the reconciliation coverage figure (88% of records reconciled) so the reader sees what the position does and does not yet cover. Middle: six domain cards (contract position, programme position, cost and cash flow, letting and procurement, design and documentation, quality), each with a status, the figure that drives it and a drill-down to the records. Bottom: the action stack, nineteen items ranked by severity, time-bar proximity and age, thirteen critical, each with its value and the source records (variation, RFI, verification check) it rests on. Right: the project analyst, a chat that answers from the records and cites them. Toggle at top left: MVP and full views, the full product being this round's enterprise build (slide 13). Not claimed: a live connection, an accuracy figure, or a lender using it; the sandbox is populated with constructed records, and real project data now comes through the R&D partnership with a SEQ Tier 2 builder (slide 2).
