Demonstration build on a Procore sandbox; the project and figures are fictional. Real project data follows through our Tier 2 R&D partner.

The screen is the proof of concept as it runs today on a populated Procore sandbox: a fictional project with a $142.5M head contract, forecast at $148.4M, reporting period May 2026. The slide shows the top of the screen: the project position (value at risk, $5.9M of forecast head-contract value; time at risk, eight weeks; cash lag, $12.9M of cost incurred and not yet recovered) and three of the six domain cards: contract position, programme position, and cost and cash flow, the three the first release takes onto live data (A12). One finding, traced. 1. The headline: eight weeks of time at risk, practical completion forecast for 9 May 2027 against 14 March 2027. 2. The programme card: the forecast runs to 96 weeks against 88 secured under the contract, so eight weeks are not covered. 3. The record: NOD-014, a notice of delay, was closed without an extension of time, so the builder’s entitlement to that time is at risk; the card links on to the time-related preliminaries the delay adds, and its drill-down opens the records behind it. For a lender, that is the completion date its facility and presale settlements depend on, and who carries the cost of the delay: the builder, if the time is not recovered, or the developer, if it is. On the contract card the same pattern shows a variation, VO-034, waiting for determination 28 days past its response window, behind $5.9M of value at risk. The full screen also holds the procurement, design and quality domains, a ranked list of actions with the source records each rests on, and a chat that answers from the records and cites them; the chat is a demonstration, and the conversational release is R4 (A12). Not claimed: a live connection, an accuracy figure, or a lender using it. The sandbox holds constructed records; real project data comes through the R&D partnership with a SEQ Tier 2 builder (slide 2, A11). The pass marks for the pilot are on A14.
